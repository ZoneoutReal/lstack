## Close / call to action
Leaders should be obligated to decide, not to stay silent. Five asks: sanction for one team workspace; a short task list plus the two untested ideas; timed tasks before and during; a named reviewer and an off-limits list; an end date and stop rule agreed before day one. Stopping is a legitimate result (#9).
