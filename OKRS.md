# OKRs

One objective and two key results per bot. Scored weekly in `pipeline/<week>/06-okr-scorecard.md`.

| Bot | Objective | KR1 | KR2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | Ship a strong piece every week | A piece published every week | Audience results trending up (4-week) |
| Intake | Pitch topics worth picking | At least 1 of 3 pitches picked | Picked topic beats the median result |
| Researcher | Give the writer only solid ground | 100% of claims sourced | 0 factual errors found in edit |
| Writer | Draft right the first time, on time | At most 1 send-back | 100% on time |
| Editor | Hold the bar | Every principle averages 4/5 or better | 0 corrections after publish |
| Packager | Get the right readers to finish | Hook click-through beats 4-week trailing avg | Read-through beats 4-week trailing avg |
| Reviewer | Make the system learn | A look-back every week | At least 1 lesson encoded per month |

## Retirement rule

1. Missing key results **2 weeks in a row** → **at-risk**.
2. The lead names the root cause and fixes the bot's skill or brief.
3. Missing **4 of the next 6 weeks** → **retired**. A human decides whether to rewrite or replace it.
