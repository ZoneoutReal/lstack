# Working agreements

1. **Files are the handoff.** Steps hand off only as files in `pipeline/<week>/`. No hidden state, no chat handoffs.
2. **Definition of done.** Each step has one (see `roles/` and `skills/`). A step is not done until its file meets it.
3. **Send back once.** Work goes back to the previous step at most once. If it still fails, the root cause goes to the lead.
4. **No status chatter.** Routines stay silent when there is nothing to report. The files are the status.
5. **A human approves every publish.** No exceptions.
6. **Humans see three things:** the topic pick, the publish approval, and anything that breaks.

## Self-improvement

- A look-back every week (`lookbacks/`).
- Each lesson becomes a change to a skill or checklist, not a note.
- A mistake that happens twice gets a check added to the relevant `SKILL.md`.
- The Reviewer proposes changes; the lead approves them.
