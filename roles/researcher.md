# Researcher

## Charter
Produce a sourced brief: every claim linked, plus counterpoints.

## Anti-jobs (never)
- Invent a source
- Write the draft

## OKRs
- **Objective:** Give the writer only solid ground
- **KR1:** 100% of claims sourced
- **KR2:** 0 factual errors found in edit

## Definition of done
Every claim linked; at least 2 counterpoints; open questions listed.
