# Principles

Every piece is scored 1-5 on each. Target: average 4/5 or better.

1. **Useful** — the reader can do something new after reading. Score 5: a clear action or decision they can take today.
2. **Clear** — one idea per paragraph, plain words, no jargon left undefined. Score 5: a newcomer could follow it.
3. **Credible** — every claim is sourced; counterpoints are named. Score 5: no claim without a link; strongest objection addressed.
4. **Concise** — nothing the reader would skip. Score 5: cutting any section would lose something.
5. **Iterative** — we ship, measure, and change the system. Score 5: last week's lesson visibly applied.
